SLIIT Coordinates Landmark Net Zero Skills Initiative for Sri Lanka

ENSURE unites local and international expertise to develop the skills, knowledge, and innovation required for Sri Lanka’s sustainable transition.

Sri Lanka's push toward a low-carbon future has taken a significant step forward with the official launch of ENSURE — Empowering Net Zero Skills for Sri Lanka's Sustainable and Resilient Future. This 2 -year, EU-funded initiative is aimed at building the workforce behind the country's 2050 net zero ambition.

"Sri Lanka's Net Zero ambition requires an equally strong investment in skills and capability” shares Dr. Jayawickrama from SLIIT’s Department of Mechanical Engineering. “ENSURE is structured to strengthen both higher education and vocational pathways, ensuring learners gain practical, future-facing expertise that supports national priorities and industry needs."

The project is coordinated by SLIIT, with Dr. Kumara Jayawickrama at the helm. It brings together a broad consortium of Sri Lankan and international partners: the Universities of Jaffna, Peradeniya, Ruhuna, and South-Eastern, alongside the Sri Lanka Energy Managers Association (SLEMA) and the Tertiary and Vocational Education Commission (TVEC). Five European universities — from Italy, Ireland, Romania, Germany, and Poland — contribute international expertise and benchmarking to the effort.

Approved with a budget of EUR 761,300 under the Erasmus+ programme, ENSURE builds on the foundation laid by the THREE-LANKA project (2021–2024), deepening its outcomes across both higher education and vocational training.

What the project will deliver:

  • Flexible, stackable master’s degree programmes in sustainability-linked fields
  • Strengthened training hubs in Carbon Capture, Hydrogen Energy, Smart Grids, and Circular Economy practices
  • Curricula aligned with the European Green Deal and COP29 outcomes
  • 1,000 individuals trained and 300 students enrolled in master's programmes

The initiative reflects a deliberate effort to connect education with real-world application — bridging industry, academia, and government to create clearer pathways from learning to employment in the green economy.
